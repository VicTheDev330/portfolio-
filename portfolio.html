I fix the thing, then write down why it broke.

Most of what I’ve learned came from servers that didn’t work. A redirect loop in WordPress, a Jenkins plugin that wouldn’t load, an Apache config that dropped sessions. Each time I traced it to the root cause and wrote it up, so the next person (often me) doesn’t repeat it.

Then I automate the fix. If I’ve done something by hand twice, it becomes a script, a playbook or a Terraform file. That habit is why my projects are measured in minutes saved and costs cut, not tools listed.
